| No. | Section | Current content | Proposed content | Reason | Proposed by |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **User Roles (Section c - Functional Requirements)** | Divided users into two broad groups: Lecturer/Admin and Student. | Split into 6 distinct roles: Student, Lecturer, Department Head, Examination Officer, Proctor, and Administrator (with standard academic offering hierarchy: Admin manages semesters/courses & assigns Department Head → Department Head assigns multiple Lecturers → Lecturer creates class section & class password + in-class shift configuring shift name, 2 anti-collision question pool auto-draw modes ALL_COURSE_BANK/SELECTED_POOL, total questions 1–10 per student, Follow-up toggle 1–5 questions, 5-minute rotating OTP, x12 parallel grading to Lecturer Dashboard; official student grades remain hidden until Lecturer audits and finalizes/publishes shift grades). | Ensure clear separation of duties across academic governance, question authoring, bank/matrix approval, shift operations & grade publishing (Examination Officer), check-in & kiosk violation logging (Proctor), grade audit (Lecturer), and 5-step internal appeals. | Nguyễn Trọng Tốt |
| **2** | **Question Bank & Approval Workflow (Section c.1 - Lecturer/Admin)** | Classified questions by subject, chapter, and difficulty level; used a single shared question pool where lecturers saved questions directly. | Classify questions by subject, Course Learning Outcome (CLO), and Bloom cognitive level (removing chapter); decouple into two independent authoring flows: the practice/mock pool (`practice_questions` by CLO) and the official exam pool (`exam_questions` strictly following the Department Head's sample exam / structure matrix); require Department Head approval of questions and 10.0-point Rubric criteria before publication. | Align with the university syllabus structure, enforce the official exam structure matrix issued by the Department Head, prevent official exam questions from leaking during student practice, and control question quality before release. | Phạm Nguyễn Đăng Hải |
| **3** | **Answer Modality & Audio Storage Policy (Sections b & c.2 - Practice & Mock Exam)** | Allowed students to answer either by voice or by typing text directly; did not separate audio storage policies between self-practice and official exams. | Clarify answer modalities: Practice mode supports flexible dual modes (direct text input or voice recording via microphone with technical term editing on the transcript); Mock Exam and Official Exam strictly enforce microphone recording (Voice-First Gate). For audio storage: Store audio recordings (Cloudflare R2) exclusively for official lab exams with a SHA-256 hash for integrity audit and appeal evidence; practice and mock exams strictly do not store audio files (Zero-R2 Invariant), storing text transcripts only. | Preserve authentic oral examination for mock and official exams, provide flexible self-study for students during practice, and optimize cloud storage costs. | Nguyễn Quang Thành |
| **4** | **Official Exam Workflow, Audit & Appeal (Section c.3 - Official Exam)** | Lecturer audited and entered scores comparing AI score and audio file; recorded exam sessions for appeal purposes. | Department Head configures exam Follow-up rules (1–5 questions, default 2); Examination Officer creates exam sessions and lab shifts (assigning both Examiner examiner_user_id and Proctor proctor_user_id), allocates official exam sets (HANDED_OVER, matching course/structure, non-duplicate in shift), and publishes the shift exam (is_exam_published = true); Proctor only checks in seats (1–40, verifying Student ID/CCCD) and logs anti-cheat violations (exam_anti_cheat_logs). Students take exams on fullscreen-locked kiosks, storing audio recordings with a SHA-256 integrity hash; background AI performs preliminary grading; Examiner audits all submissions; upon passing Publish Hard Gate (100% graded/accounted for, zero pending/DLQ, mandatory audit for suspicious/<0.70 confidence cases), Examiner or Examination Officer publishes grades once, activating One-Way Lock (is_locked = true). 5-step appeal workflow: Student views published grade to acknowledge (ACKNOWLEDGED) or submit appeal (AppealRequest) → Examination Officer and Department Head receive notifications; Examination Officer verifies eligibility and assigns independent Lecturer to regrade (excluding original Examiner, hard quota <= 24) → Lecturer regrades → Examination Officer approves final decision (APPROVED/REJECTED), unlocking One-Way Lock to update official score. | Standardize centralized computer lab examination at FPTU, enforce clear separation of powers among Examination Officer, Proctor, Department Head, and Lecturer, and ensure transparency via the 5-step appeal workflow. | Lê Vũ Hoàng |
| **5** | **Advanced Features Scope & Account Provisioning (Section c.4 - Advanced Features)** | Listed candidate extensions: English pronunciation/fluency scoring, AI follow-up generation, tab-switch/camera detection, and live video call oral exams. | Retain and implement officially: contextual AI follow-up questions, focus-loss/fullscreen violation enforcement in computer labs, and CLO-based analytics. Move Out of Scope: live Video Call integration, webcam proctoring, English pronunciation scoring, and public account self-registration (only university-provisioned accounts are used). | Official exams are conducted on-site in university computer labs under direct Proctor supervision rather than via remote Video Calls; courses assess Software Engineering domain knowledge in Vietnamese with English technical terms rather than foreign-language pronunciation. | Nguyễn Quang Thành |
| **6** | **Grading Execution & Fault-Tolerant Processing (Section d - Non-Functional Requirements)** | Invoked LLM grading directly during request wait; saved answers on LLM error or timeout for later regrading. | Decouple submission recording from automated grading: the server persists student submissions to the database immediately before queuing background grading, combined with 3x automatic retries and a Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ), where Admin monitors/replays practice & mock exam DLQ and Examination Officer monitors/replays official exam DLQ. | Ensure the exam interface in computer labs responds immediately without freezing or losing submissions during AI overload, while enforcing proper role separation for DLQ recovery between Admin and Examination Officer. | Nguyễn Trọng Tốt |
