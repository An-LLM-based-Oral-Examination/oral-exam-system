PHASE 1: QUESTION AUTHORING & RUBRIC INVARIANT GUARD (Σ ≡ 10.0 pts)
Invariant Rule: Total sum of Rubric criteria points must strictly equal 10.0 pts
STEP 1
Sign In & RBAC
Authenticates JWT token with Lecturer claims (Role = lecturer | department_head | admin) into Authoring Studio.
Open Studio
STEP 2
3-Tier Filter Structure
Filters: Course → Scope (Practice / Mock / Official / Shared) → Bloom Level 1-6 (by Bloom & CLO matrix).
Draft Question
STEP 3
Question & Model Answer
Enters oral exam prompt, Bloom taxonomy difficulty, and benchmark Model Answer (≥ 50 chars) for LLM reference.
Assign Rubric
STEP 4
Configure Criteria
Specifies criteria items C1..Ck: Concept, Application, Trade-off Analysis & assigned point weights.
Compute Σ Sum
STEP 5 (GUARD)
Client Invariant Σ ≡ 10.0 pts
• Mismatch (Σ≠10): Red alert & Lock Save button.
• Valid (Σ=10): Unlocks simulation & persistence.
AUTHORING PATHS
Authoring Strategy?
Lecturer uses AI generation from FLM with custom rubric & Dept review, or dry-run simulation / direct save.
BRANCH 1 · AI GEN FLM & DEPT REVIEW
Lecturer AI FLM Gen & Dept Approval
AI generates from FLM with custom 10.0 rubric; Lecturer submits to Department for 3 decisions.
STEP 6A
AI Gen FLM & Custom Rubric
Gemini generates by CLO & Bloom; Custom Rubric Σ=10.0 pts; Model Answer ≥ 50 chars.
STEP 7A
Submit to Department Head
Lecturer tunes in Preview Studio, clicks "Submit to Department" (SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW).
STEP 8A (3 DECISIONS)
Dept Head Decision Gate
• APPROVED: Official ACID commit
• NEEDS_REVISION: Return with notes
• REJECTED: Discard question
BRANCH 2 · AI SIMULATOR
AI Calibration Simulator (Dry-run)
Inputs candidate sample answers (Good/Avg/Poor) to test rubric sensitivity before saving.
STEP 6B
Input Sample & AI Grade
Gemini Flash performs dry-run evaluation, outputs vector subscores and CoT critique.
STEP 7B
Review & Weight Tuning
Instructor inspects result: fine-tunes criteria weights or keeps intact for final commit.
STEP 8B
Backend Gate & ACID
FluentValidation checks Σ=10.0m (422 if invalid); ACID commits Question + Rubric + AuditLog.
BRANCH 3 · DIRECT SAVE
Direct Save (Skip Simulator)
Used when rubric is pre-validated or for minor copyedits; submits payload directly to backend.
STEP 6C
Skip Simulator & Submit
Instructor clicks Save; bundles Question, Criteria Σ=10.0 pts and Model Answer ≥ 50 chars.
STEP 8C
Backend Gate HTTP 422 & ACID
Independent Σ=10.0m validation; executes PostgreSQL 16+ transaction & marks status ACTIVE.
